# Brand review for the public development build

Reviewed: 2026-11-27

- The configured Windows, macOS, and in-editor app icons use Brickwright robot artwork. The in-editor icon reviewed by SHA-256 is `static/images/apple-touch-icon.png` (`717cacb3aca03ea5453143b6833770b557cc4a768a62529503f571b9d020035e`).
- The menu bar previously replaced its logo with Scratch Cat and retro Scratch marks in optional time-travel modes. Those logo imports, replacement branches, and unused logo files were removed; the visual time-travel filters remain.
- `desktop/about.html` identifies Brickwright as independent, states that LEGO Group, Scratch Foundation, and TurboWarp do not develop, approve, or sponsor it, and includes the Scratch Foundation attribution requested by its current source-code terms.
- The public release notes and `TRADEMARK` file identify LEGO/SPIKE and Scratch marks as belonging to their respective owners and disclaim official affiliation.
